Scientific information about autism — in Kinyarwanda and English. Ending stigma starts with understanding.

Kinyarwanda

Autisme ni iki?

Autisme ni imiterere y'ubwonko yihariye ituma umuntu abona isi mu buryo butandukanye. Si indwara, si igitugu, kandi si ikimenyetso cy'ababyeyi bakora nabi. Ni uburyo bwa kamere mu buvandimwe bw'ubwonko bwa muntu.
🌍
English

What is autism?

Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a natural variation in how the brain works. It affects how people communicate, interact socially, and process sensory information. It is not caused by bad parenting, vaccines, or spiritual problems.
⏰
English

Why early detection matters

Children who receive support before age 3 have significantly better outcomes in communication, independence, and learning. Early screening is the first step to getting a child the support they deserve — and Ijwi makes that possible from any location in Rwanda.

English

Different ability, not disability

Many autistic people have exceptional abilities in memory, pattern recognition, mathematics, and creativity. The goal is not to "fix" autism — it is to provide the right support so every child can reach their full potential on their own terms.
🏥

Where to get help in Rwanda

Rwanda Biomedical Centre (RBC) — national authority for child health screening.

Kacyiru Hospital — child development unit, Kigali.

Your local community health worker — can help you use the Ijwi app and submit a referral.
